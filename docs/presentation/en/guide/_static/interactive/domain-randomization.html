1. Vary conditions

Choose conditions that may vary in deployment, such as vial placement or lighting. A reset samples new conditions within the configured ranges. Wider ranges can create implausible scenes or make collection harder; inspect both camera views and keep the task feasible.

Inspect the enabled reset parameters

2. Record examples

Collect successful demonstrations across those resets. The recorded images, joint states, and actions become learning examples. A visual change reaches training only when it appears in the data or a training-time transform. Saving a take does not reset the scene; the lesson uses R for a fresh setup.

Collect and review demonstrations

3. Post-train

Post-training adapts GR00T 1.7 using windows from the reviewed demonstrations. Simulation randomization changes the environment in which examples are collected. Image transforms vary training images but do not create new motion demonstrations. More repeated examples cannot supply a missing approach or recovery.

Connect the data to post-training

4. Test transfer

Compare policies under documented evaluation conditions. Keep the checkpoint, instruction, action horizon, and relevant starting conditions traceable. A stronger simulation score does not establish real-robot success. Verify the intended and achieved start against the actual training data before a supervised physical rollout.

Record evaluation outcomes
