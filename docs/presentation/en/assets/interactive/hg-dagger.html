1. Watch the policy

A policy can reach states that successful demonstrations rarely show: a missed grasp, an awkward approach, or a partial lift. HG-DAgger collects a useful human action at those policy-visited states. Observe the failure before deciding what correction to demonstrate.

Understand the correction loop

2. Pause and align

Pause policy control and align the leader with the follower before a correction. The physical leader moves under motor power during startup, alignment, resets, and exit, including with a simulated follower. Keep its path clear and support it before releasing torque. Follow the lesson's alignment controls and wait for correction_ready.

Follow the safe handoff sequence

3. Record a correction

Guide a useful grasp, lift, or placement and save the correction. The workshop records saved human correction segments, not the preceding autonomous attempt, pause, or alignment. Return control to the policy when appropriate. The policy weights stay fixed throughout collection.

Check what becomes a training episode

4. Post-train offline

Review corrections, combine them with original demonstrations, and run a separate post-training job. Keeping examples of motions that already work helps test whether the updated policy retains those skills. Corrections supply action targets; this collection process does not update weights online or run reinforcement learning.

5. Reevaluate unaided

Evaluate the updated policy without human help. Assisted collection successes are not autonomous evaluation successes. Use compatible inputs and the documented horizon, verify training-data start coverage and the achieved pose, and test new vial positions. Record the full task outcome and whether earlier skills still work.
